Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description:

Warm, gooey, and golden-brown cookies packed with rich chocolate chips. Crispy on the edges and soft in the center, they are pure homemade heaven!

Ingredients:

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated white sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs & 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:

  1. Prep dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Cream butter and sugars: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add wet ingredients: Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Combine and mix: Gradually blend the flour mixture into the butter mixture, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Bake: Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto a baking sheet and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 9–11 minutes until golden brown. Cool before enjoying!
